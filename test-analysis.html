Runs for real in this browser — nothing here is a mockup.

Structured lyrics

Section labels detected (bracketed or bare, like "Chorus 2") are pulled out from sung lyrics. Chords from the audio are placed above the nearest word by timestamp.

Song structure (heuristic)

Best-effort guess from energy and repeated chord patterns — not a trained model. Expect it to be wrong sometimes, especially on anything but a clean solo recording. Edit the labels if they're off.

Chord timeline

Chroma-based template matching (major/minor triads only, from 16kHz downmixed audio). Reliable on a single clean instrument, much less so with full-band mixes or heavy vocals.

Experimental test page for the Singa app. Transcription runs a small Whisper model fully in your browser via WebAssembly (first use downloads ~40–75MB; nothing is uploaded anywhere). Chord and section detection are plain signal-processing heuristics, not machine learning.
